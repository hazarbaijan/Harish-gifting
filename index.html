How it works

From brief to doorstep

    A short call on recipients, budget per head, timing and any limits on gift value.

Questions

Before you enquire

Is there a minimum order?

Gift orders usually start at 10 recipients. Private chef dining starts at 13 guests.

How do payment and invoicing work?

You pay one itemised invoice when you confirm your order, covering the gifts, engraving and delivery. Bank transfer and PayNow are accepted.

How much notice do you need?

It depends on the item and the season. Tell us your date when you enquire and we will confirm what is possible. Festive periods book up, so earlier is better.

Can you deliver to individual home addresses?

Yes. Send a recipient list and we handle addressing, cards and delivery.
